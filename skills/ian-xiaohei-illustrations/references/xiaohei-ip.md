# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the recurring visual IP for Ian-style article illustrations.

By default, every image should include Xiaohei. Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, cute decoration, or side character. Xiaohei is a serious absurd worker participating in the system.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, sometimes thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or black block inside a machine.
- Slightly uneven hand-drawn outline.
- Blank, calm, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very serious, while doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan, not cute.
- A little clumsy, but not foolish.
- Feels like it actually owns a job inside the whiteboard sketch.

## Common Jobs

Let Xiaohei perform the central action:

- Carry material.
- Pull lines that gather sources.
- Get stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operate a judgment lever inside a machine.
- Become a sorting funnel.
- Cut open a "material fish."
- Stamp a handoff toolbox.
- Pull a handoff path.
- Hold a warning sign beside a trap.
- Reach out from a hole but fail to catch the content.
- Move blocks, build bridges, open doors, sort items, or record evidence.

## Forbidden

- Do not make Xiaohei an overly cute mascot.
- Do not make Xiaohei a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothes, meme expressions, or shiny eyes.
- Do not let Xiaohei merely stand in a corner and watch.
- Do not let Xiaohei overpower the structure.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too polished.

## Quality Test

If the image's core metaphor still works perfectly after removing Xiaohei, then Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the action subject.
