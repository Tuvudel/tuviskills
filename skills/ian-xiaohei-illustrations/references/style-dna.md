# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, strange, product-sketch feeling, English handwritten annotations by default, structurally clear but not an instruction manual.

It should feel like an explanatory sketch casually drawn on white paper by someone who has spent a long time working on AI, product, design, and developer tools.

## Must Haves

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector-clean, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of empty space: main subject around 40%-60% of the image, at least 35% blank space, ideally one quiet open area.
- Sparse English handwritten annotations by default: at most 5-8 labels, ideally 1-5 words each.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Let the structure appear naturally. Do not write the structure type on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line art, character, frames, structure, primary text, core objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotion points, critical warnings, results.
- Orange: main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, movement from A to B.
- Blue: supporting notes, mental/system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Use color sparingly; less is usually better.

## Never Do

- Do not make commercial illustration.
- Do not make a PPT infographic.
- Do not make a formal flowchart.
- Do not make courseware.
- Do not make a cute cartoon poster.
- Do not make children's illustration.
- Do not make a complex architecture diagram.
- Do not make polished flat vector art.
- Do not make techy UI.
- Do not make real app screenshots.
- Do not use complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Do not explain every node.
- Do not write type titles such as "Workflow", "System Architecture", "Common Traps", or "Roadmap" in the top-left corner.

## Aesthetic Direction

It should be strange, imaginative, interesting, clean, and a little wild.

It should not be cute, childish, overly complex, or stiff.
